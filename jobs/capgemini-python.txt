Your role

As a Python Backend Developer , you will contribute to the design, development, and maintenance of scalable backend applications and services that power business-critical platforms and digital solutions. Working alongside experienced engineers and cross-functional teams, you will help build reliable, high-performance systems with a focus on clean code, automation, and continuous improvement.

In this role you will play a key role in:

Developing and maintaining backend applications using Python
Designing and implementing RESTful APIs and microservices
Building scalable and secure backend solutions that support business requirements
Integrating applications with databases, third-party services, and internal systems
Writing clean, maintainable, and well-tested code following software engineering best practices
Collaborating with frontend developers, product owners, and other stakeholders to deliver end-to-end solutions
Troubleshooting, optimizing, and enhancing existing applications and services
Contributing to CI/CD processes, automation initiatives, and cloud-based deployments
Participating in code reviews and supporting continuous improvement of development practices
Your profile:

Experience with Python development
Good understanding of object-oriented programming and software design principles
Experience building and consuming REST APIs
Knowledge of SQL and relational databases
Experience working with Git and version control workflows
Familiarity with backend frameworks such as FastAPI, Flask, or Django
Strong problem-solving and analytical skills
Good communication skills and ability to work in a collaborative environment
