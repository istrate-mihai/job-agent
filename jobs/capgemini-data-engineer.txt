Junior Data Engineer
At Capgemini Engineering, the world leader in engineering services, we bring together a global team of engineers, scientists, and architects to help the world's most innovative companies unleash their potential. From autonomous cars to life-saving robots, our digital and software technology experts think outside the box as they provide unique R&D and engineering services across all industries. Join us for a career full of opportunities. Where you can make a difference. Where no two days are the same.

Your role As a Data Engineer, you will contribute to the development and maintenance of modern data platforms and data pipelines that support business reporting, analytics, and operational processes. Working alongside experienced engineers and stakeholders, you will help build scalable solutions for processing, transforming, and managing data in cloud environments.

In this role you will play a key role in:

Developing and maintaining data pipelines using Python and AWS services
Supporting ETL/ELT workflows for ingesting, transforming, and loading data from multiple sources
Working with structured and semi-structured datasets to ensure data quality and consistency Writing efficient SQL queries and supporting data model design activities
Collaborating with engineering, analytics, and business teams to understand data requirements
Monitoring and troubleshooting data pipelines and production data issues
Contributing to CI/CD processes and automation activities related to data platforms Supporting cloud-based data solutions and participating in continuous improvement initiatives
Your profile:

Experience with Python development
Good knowledge of SQL and relational databases
Hands-on experience with AWS services (such as S3, Lambda, Glue, Redshift, Athena, or similar)
Understanding of ETL/ELT concepts and data processing workflows
Familiarity with data pipeline development and maintenance
Experience working with version control systems such as Git
Good analytical and problem-solving skills
Strong communication skills and willingness to learn in a collaborative environment
Nice to have:

Experience with Apache Airflow
Knowledge of Spark or PySpark
Exposure to Data Warehousing concepts Experience with Docker and CI/CD pipelines Familiarity with dbt, Databricks, or cloud-native data platforms
